SPROUT PAL - put it on your iPhone

1. Go to github.com and create a free account.
2. Click "+" (top right) > New repository. Name it sproutpal, set it to Public, click Create.
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in everything from this folder:
   index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, and the icons folder. Click Commit changes.
4. Open Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" pick main, then Save.
   After a minute or two your app is live at:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/sproutpal/
5. On your iPhone, open that link in Safari. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.

Open Sprout Pal from the home-screen icon from now on. That copy keeps your data.
Your entries stay on your phone only. Use Rewards > Save backup every week or two.

Updating later: upload the changed files to the same repository, and change
"sproutpal-v1" to "sproutpal-v2" (and so on) at the top of sw.js so phones pick up the new version.
